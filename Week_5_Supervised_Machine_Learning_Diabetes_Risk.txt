    "## 3. Identify target and features\n",
    "\n",
    "The target/output is `Outcome`. The predictors are the available clinical and demographic measurements. In this dataset, zero values in Glucose, BloodPressure, SkinThickness, Insulin, and BMI can represent missing measurements; those zeros are changed to missing values before imputation. Pregnancies may legitimately be zero."

  {
   "cell_type": "code",
   "execution_count": null,
   "id": "61b44117",
   "metadata": {},
   "outputs": [],
   "source": [
    "target_col = \"Outcome\"\n",
    "candidate_features = [\n",
    "    \"Pregnancies\", \"Glucose\", \"BloodPressure\", \"SkinThickness\",\n",
    "    \"Insulin\", \"BMI\", \"DiabetesPedigreeFunction\", \"Age\"\n",
    "]\n",
    "feature_cols = [col for col in candidate_features if col in df.columns]\n",
    "\n",
    "if target_col not in df.columns:\n",
    "    raise ValueError(\"The dataset must contain an 'Outcome' target column.\")\n",
    "if not feature_cols:\n",
    "    raise ValueError(\"No expected predictor columns were found; inspect df.columns.\")\n",
    "\n",
    "model_data = df[feature_cols + [target_col]].copy()\n",
    "for col in feature_cols + [target_col]:\n",
    "    model_data[col] = pd.to_numeric(model_data[col], errors=\"coerce\")\n",
    "\n",
    "for col in [\"Glucose\", \"BloodPressure\", \"SkinThickness\", \"Insulin\", \"BMI\"]:\n",
    "    if col in model_data.columns:\n",
    "        model_data.loc[model_data[col] == 0, col] = np.nan\n",
    "\n",
    "model_data = model_data.dropna(subset=[target_col])\n",
    "model_data = model_data[model_data[target_col].isin([0, 1])]\n",
    "X = model_data[feature_cols]\n",
    "y = model_data[target_col].astype(int)\n",
    "\n",
    "print(\"Target/output:\", target_col)\n",
    "print(\"Features:\", feature_cols)\n",
    "print(\"Class counts (0 = no diabetes recorded, 1 = diabetes recorded):\")\n",
    "display(y.value_counts().sort_index().rename(\"count\").to_frame())\n",
    "print(\"Usable rows:\", len(model_data))\n",

    "logistic_model = Pipeline(steps=[\n",
    "    (\"imputer\", SimpleImputer(strategy=\"median\")),\n",

    "    (\"model\", LogisticRegression(max_iter=2000, random_state=RANDOM_STATE))\n",

    "\n",
    "forest_model = Pipeline(steps=[\n",
    "    (\"imputer\", SimpleImputer(strategy=\"median\")),\n",
    "    (\"model\", RandomForestClassifier(\n",
    "        n_estimators=300,\n",
    "        random_state=RANDOM_STATE,\n",
    "        min_samples_leaf=3,\n",
    "        class_weight=\"balanced\"\n",
    "    ))\n",
    "])\n",
    "\n",
    "logistic_model.fit(X_train, y_train)\n",
    "forest_model.fit(X_train, y_train)\n",

    "## 7. Evaluate model performance\n",
    "\n",
    "We use:\n",
    "- **Accuracy:** proportion of all predictions that are correct.\n",
    "- **Precision:** proportion of predicted positive cases that are truly positive.\n",
    "- **Recall:** proportion of actual positive cases detected by the model.\n",
    "- **F1-score:** harmonic mean of precision and recall.\n",
    "- **ROC-AUC:** how well predicted probabilities rank positive cases above negative cases.\n",
    "\n",
    "For a health-related screening context, recall can be important because false negatives may miss people who need further assessment. No metric alone is enough to judge clinical usefulness."
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "code",
   "execution_count": null,
   "id": "8017fcd1",
   "metadata": {},
   "outputs": [],
   "source": [
    "metric_rows = []\n",
    "for name, pred in predictions.items():\n",
    "    row = {\n",
    "        \"Accuracy\": accuracy_score(y_test, pred),\n",
    "        \"Precision\": precision_score(y_test, pred, zero_division=0),\n",
    "        \"Recall\": recall_score(y_test, pred, zero_division=0),\n",
    "        \"F1-score\": f1_score(y_test, pred, zero_division=0),\n",
    "        \"ROC-AUC\": roc_auc_score(y_test, probabilities[name])\n",
    "    }\n",
    "    metric_rows.append(pd.Series(row, name=name))\n",
    "\n",
    "metrics = pd.DataFrame(metric_rows)\n",
    "display(metrics.round(4))"
   ]
